Your ads × your flow

Keep the promise the ad made. Two of your Meta ads, two flows, side by side.

Your Meta ads that link to this app each sell one thing: swap an outfit, turn a photo into a video, a family photoshoot. In the flow we have on record, every one of those installs meets the same start: a sign-up screen, then a paywall about “Better designs in less time” over sneaker mockups.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by where the install came from, so the first screens repeat what the ad promised, and each ad is judged on the subscriptions it brings, not the installs.

Day 1

Your name, checked

You show on 40% of US Apple Ads searches for “picsart”. Check who shows first on the rest, and take the top slot back.

Week 1

Two ad flows live

After a one-time SDK setup, each ad opens its own flow, set by where the install came from. No app release.

Week 2

A/B tests per ad

Each ad flow against one flow for everyone, measured on subscriptions, not installs. Then the family photoshoot ad gets its own first screen.

Both ads link to the App Store, to this app. Other Meta ads of yours go to the web: several to your web funnel on picsart.fnlfx.com, and the AI Playground, Flow and MCP ads to picsart.com. On Apple Ads, in the US data we see for 9 Oct – 9 Nov, you show on 40% of searches for “picsart”, so the rest go to other apps, and you also bid on “photo” (37%), “cutout” (58%) and two Canva names (1%). These figures are for your account; we can’t tell which of your apps each ad was for. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
